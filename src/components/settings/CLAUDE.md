# src/components/settings — the settings dialog

The settings dialog and the folder-grant form. Also owns `src/lib/`'s `sandbox-*.tsx` and `permission-settings.tsx`. The webview-wide rules, security invariants first, are [`src/CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md), which also covers the color-scheme picker (*Theming & storage*).

**The Settings dialog** is `components/settings/settings-dialog/`, exporting `SettingsDialog` alone: a `PanelDialog` (`primitives/dialog.tsx`) with vertical tabs beside its pane. It opens on General (`general.tsx`), which holds the Mode picker (`color-scheme-picker.tsx`); a tab with no `content` says *Coming soon*. A hidden tab's pane unmounts, so its watches run only while it is selected.

**Clusters** (`clusters.tsx`) lists `useClusters()`'s kubeconfig clusters, then the archived ones (no longer in the kubeconfig), hidden when there are none; nothing draws before the `Bookmark`. Every string from a cluster goes through `VisibleText`. Its actions are `ClusterSyncPanel`'s mutations (`src/lib/cluster-mutations.ts`), with no confirm; each row redraws from the watch.

**Sandbox and Permissions are not reachable from Settings for now.** Their sections below wait for their tabs. Each draws only while `useSandbox` answers `available: true` and has one hook for its queries and mutations. Every path, target, context, pattern and rule line goes through `VisibleText`. A hook re-asks its query after each mutation and on focus where the query alone carries state another window can change.

- **`PATH`** (`settings-dialog/sandbox.tsx`, `useSandboxPath` in `src/lib/sandbox-path.tsx`). Entries keep whitespace and spell trailing characters, since Include approves the folder drawn. Include sends the drawn `target`, so a refresh that moved it refuses. While the fault is set, what commands search comes from `sandboxPathResolved`, never from the list's length. → [ADR: `PATH` is the login shell's, filtered and frozen](../../../docs/adr/2026-10-02-path-is-the-login-shells-filtered-and-frozen.md).
- **Executables** (`SandboxExecutableList`, `useSandboxExecutables` in `src/lib/sandbox-executables.tsx`) over the `sandboxExecutablesWatch` gauge. The probe mutation answers at once and the watch carries the end. After a press, *Probe again* holds until the watch's `probes` count passes the one seen at the press — the answer and the frame race, and a gauge can fold a short probe's start into its end.
- **Folders** (`useSandboxFolders(chatID?)` in `src/lib/sandbox-folders.tsx`; `grant` runs under `chatGrantsContext`). **`FolderGrantForm`** (`components/settings/folder-grant-form.tsx`) is the one form a folder is granted from, here and in the transcript; it sends the path exactly as typed. Remove is disabled while `rulesHeld`. *Never readable* is read-only. → [path grants](../../../docs/security/2026-10-04-path-grants.md).
- **Permissions** (`settings-dialog/permissions.tsx`, `usePermissionSettings` in `src/lib/permission-settings.tsx`). It re-asks `permissionSettings` on every opening and on focus. **The hook alone spells the wire's dotted keys** (`permissions.defaultMode`, `.modes`, `.rules`); everything else uses `PermissionField`. While the file holds values Kstack cannot read, that field's edits are disabled and *Discard what Kstack cannot read* (`permissionDiscardRefused`) sits behind a confirm naming them. *Clear* acts on a context's own entry, never a pattern it matches. → [the permissions engine](../../../docs/security/2026-10-02-the-permissions-engine.md).
